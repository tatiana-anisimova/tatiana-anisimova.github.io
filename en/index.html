Irisk · social project

Act Beautifully

Production producer

Production: Debosh

Russian professional hand and nail care brand Irisk turned its attention to how often hands become the centre of attention. The project features the hands of deaf girls who invite us to act beautifully, not just talk about it.

For World AIDS Day

Positive People

Production producer

Production: Debosh

The project helps challenge the stigma around people living with HIV: it explains how the virus is transmitted, how to prevent it and how to interact safely. It includes a leaflet and posters, plus an interview with a serodiscordant couple.
